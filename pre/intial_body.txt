Specific Part / System	Location	Role / "Use Case" in Thinking, Feeling, and Acting
Frontal Lobe	Front of the cerebrum (behind forehead)	The Manager: Handles conscious choice, logic, complex planning, self-control, personality, and physical speech production.
Temporal Lobe	Sides of the cerebrum (by the ears)	The Audio & Memory Hub: Processes hearing, understands spoken language, and houses the pathways to store long-term memories. (IN THIS PLEASE LEAVE THE AUDIO PART FOR NOW WE WILL DO IT LATER)
Limbic System	Deep inner center of the brain	The Emotional Engine: Generates raw emotions (fear, joy, anger), links feelings to memories, and triggers survival instincts.
The Gut (ENS)	Gastrointestinal tract / Stomach	The Mood Regulator: Houses millions of nerves and produces 90% of your body's serotonin; heavily controls baseline anxiety and "gut instincts."
Adrenal Glands	On top of the kidneys	The Action Switch: Floods the bloodstream with adrenaline and cortisol to physically launch the body into an active survival mode.
Thyroid Gland	Front of the neck	The Brain's Throttle: Sets the baseline metabolic speed of your brain cells. Imbalances directly cause severe brain fog, depression, or panic.
