--- Kuru Dynasty (Mahabharata / Gita Family Tree) ---
Dynasty: Kuru Dynasty
- King Shantanu married Ganga -> Son: Bhishma (vowed celibacy, never married).
- King Shantanu married Satyavati -> Sons: 
  * Chitrangada (died early without heirs)
  * Vichitravirya (married Ambika & Ambalika, died childless)
- Sage Vyasa (Satyavati's son from previous union) fathered the next generation through Niyoga:
  * Dhritarashtra (mother Ambika): Succeeded by 100 sons (The Kauravas, eldest Duryodhana, second Dushasana, third Vikarna) and 1 daughter (Dushala, married Jayadratha). Also fathered Yuyutsu with Sughada (a Vaishya maid).
  * Pandu (mother Ambalika): Married Kunti and Madri.
    - Kunti's son before marriage: Karna (divine father Surya, raised by Adhiratha & Radha).
    - Kunti's sons: Yudhishthira (father Dharma), Bhima (father Vayu), Arjuna (father Indra).
    - Madri's sons: Nakula & Sahadeva (father Ashwini Kumaras).
  * Vidura (mother Parishrami/maidservant): Prime Minister of Hastinapur.
- Third Generation:
  * Common wife of Pandavas: Draupadi.
  * Upapandavas (sons of Draupadi): Prativindhya (with Yudhishthira), Sutasoma (with Bhima), Srutakarma (with Arjuna), Shatanika (with Nakula), Srutasena (with Sahadeva).
  * Other prominent children:
    - Abhimanyu (father Arjuna, mother Subhadra).
    - Ghatotkacha (father Bhima, mother Hidimba).
    - Iravan (father Arjuna, mother Ulupi).
    - Babruvahana (father Arjuna, mother Chitrangada of Manipur).
- Fourth Generation & Succession:
  * Parikshit (father Abhimanyu, mother Uttara): Succeeded Yudhishthira to the throne.
  * Janamejaya (son of Parikshit): Performed the Sarpa Satra (snake sacrifice).
